MATCHING:

Each task (param setting x project case) takes a very variable amount of time,
because genetic matching is much much slower than CEM or NN matching, especially
for large values of pop.size (~30 minutes vs 5 seconds)

This makes within-job parallelization inefficient for jobs with a smaller number
of tasks (a couple cores get stuck with finishing long computations while the
others are idle), but many tasks per job results in longer jobs where monitoring
and debugging is less flexible. 

Therefore I recommend something like: 10 tasks per job, 2 hr 15 minutes per job,
just one core per job with 2 GB. 



SYNTHETIC CONTROLS:

Again, each task takes a variable amount of time, with augsynth taking
significantly longer than gsynth or microsynth depending on what method is used
for the outcome model correction (seq-to-seq being the longest). 

I recommend (for the current parameter grid): 15 tasks per job, 12 hrs per job,
one core per job with 4 GB. Some jobs will finish much quicker than 12 hrs - 
there may be a better way to distribute tasks across jobs to prevent this. For
now, always include sc_method and progfunc as time_vars when creating the
parameter grid.
